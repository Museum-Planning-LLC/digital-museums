Digital Museum Services.

COVID-19 changed museums permanently. The shift to digital is now necessary for museums to stay relevant and sustainable.

Museum directors, board members, staff, city managers, and mayors need to understand how and why museums have changed in order to connect with their audiences and build sustainable museums.

Museum Planning LLC plans and creates digital experiences for museum audiences, online, in person, and both at once.
