The Book

Digital Museums 101.

A how-to guide for museum directors, board members, staff, and students on how digital media has changed museum exhibitions, marketing, and fundraising.

Mark Walhimer
DigitalMuseums 101
Museum Planning LLC
digital-museums.com

Digital media has changed the way museums create exhibitions, market themselves, and raise funds. The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated those changes, including the shift to online programming. Using diagrams and an iconography system, the book explains how to build digital programming and sustainable operations.

It also looks at the history and the future of museums' shift toward digital content and communication. Generation Z and Generation Alpha are the first digital natives: they perceive and process content differently, and museums must adapt to reach them.

For anyone new to museums, and for anyone catching up on how digital content has changed exhibitions, marketing, and fundraising, Digital Museums 101 is essential reading.

What it covers.

  • Digital museum management
  • Museum digital exhibitions — online, in-person, and mixed
  • Digital museum marketing
  • Digital museum fundraising
  • Digital museum operations
